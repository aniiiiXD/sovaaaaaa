# Market brief design

Subject: an internal founder brief for a custom-model company inspired by Dvar, Hyde and Isoquant. Explain what to sell, who buys, market-sizing assumptions and delivery economics.

Palette: cloud #f6f8fc, ink #202b49, violet #6657d5, lilac #e9e7fa, sea glass #d6eadf, line #d9deea. Avenir Next display and system sans body; no external assets or dependencies. Large left-aligned headings, constrained prose, generous section spacing, differentiated diagrams and interactive controls.

Layout: compact navigation → two-column thesis + training-loop illustration → three company reference rows → selectable customer profiles → interactive TAM/SAM/capacity model → payback chart → commercial offer → funding evidence and sources. Narrow screens stack columns and retain readable chart labels.

Review before build: rejected a black dashboard, generic KPI-card hero and speculative company scatterplot. The distinctive element is a schematic of the work being sold. Market size is explicitly an editable scenario; sourced spending anchors sit separately. No numeric company difficulty/ROI rankings. Illustrative buyers are not claimed customers.

Inspection: open the standalone file in Chrome, inspect desktop and narrow layout, check profile buttons and market/ROI controls, verify downloadable SVG and saved assumptions where feasible. No ML code is executed.

## Validation result

The browser security policy rejected the local file URL. No alternate browser route was used, and no visual inspection is claimed. Local Node checks passed for JavaScript syntax, unique IDs and internal anchors, market default/preset calculations, the SAM capacity cap, all customer-profile handlers, and payback handling for positive savings, equal costs, higher costs and invalid input. A numeric-input step mismatch was corrected before the final check. These checks do not validate rendering or browser downloads.
